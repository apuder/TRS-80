Privacy Policy — TRS-80 for iOS

Last updated 6 August 2026. This policy covers the TRS-80 app for iPhone and iPad. The Android version of the app is covered separately.

The short version

The app collects nothing about you. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no account to create and nothing to buy. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere, and no identifier for you or your device is created, stored or transmitted.

What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers is stored on the device itself, inside the app's own storage, and is never uploaded:

  • the machines you set up — their names, models and settings
  • disk images and cassette files you add or create
  • saved sessions, so a machine resumes where you left it
  • the screenshot shown for each machine in your library
  • your app settings, such as the theme

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

When the app uses the network

The app makes network requests only to fetch things you asked for. It sends no personal data with them, and no identifier is attached.

  • RetroStore (retrostore.org) — the catalog of programs, and a program's files when you choose to run it.
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) — the two ROM images the emulator needs, downloaded once on first run.
  • The App Store (apps.apple.com) — opened only if you tap “Rate this app”.

As with any request over the internet, the servers answering these will see the connection itself, including the IP address it came from, because that is how a reply finds its way back. Those services are operated by others and handle their own logs under their own policies. The app sends them nothing about you beyond the request.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, export and erasure apply to personal data held about you. None is held: there is no account, no profile and no server-side record of you. The data described above is on your device and under your control, and uninstalling the app erases it.

Changes

If a future version of the app starts collecting anything — crash reporting is the likeliest candidate — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.

Contact
